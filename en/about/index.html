Uğrak / About us

About us

Uğrak is a small city journal brand founded in Sinop, on Türkiye's Black Sea coast, by Aylin Tok. In Turkish, uğramak means to stop by on your way and really look. Our journals exist to help travellers look at a city again.

What we make

Digital city journals for Sinop, Amasra, Cappadocia and Safranbolu. Each opens like an app on your phone, with place pages, walking routes, audio stories in English and Turkish, small quests and your own travel diary.

How we research

  • We research every city from scratch, using municipal, museum and ministry sources, academic publications and magazines.
  • Dates, measurements and names are checked against sources. If we can't confirm something, we leave it out or clearly label it as a legend.
  • Legend pages name their source and note that they are folk tales, not history.
  • Every text gets a second review before publishing. For things that change, such as opening hours and fees, we tell you to check before you go.
  • Spotted a mistake? Tell us and we'll fix it and update the "last checked" date.

Where we use AI

  • Illustrations: created with AI tools, then selected and edited by us.
  • Audio narration: the narrator's voice is AI-generated; we write and check the scripts.
  • Texts: drafted with the help of AI tools; every fact is checked against sources and edited by us.

We use AI as a tool in our process; research, selection, fact-checking, editing and the final decision to publish are ours. We say this openly because trusting a guide starts with knowing how it was made.
