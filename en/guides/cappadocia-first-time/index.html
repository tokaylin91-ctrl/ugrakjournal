Cappadocia for first-time visitors

Short answer: For a first visit, base yourself around Göreme and Uçhisar and allow at least three days: one for the rock churches and the Open-Air Museum, one for the Pigeon Valley walk to Uçhisar, and one for Love Valley and a quiet village afternoon. Balloons depend on the weather; bring water and a hat, and never climb the fairy chimneys.

Where to focus

Cappadocia is a large region, and trying to see all of it on a first trip is tiring. The Göreme and Uçhisar area, with its fairy chimneys, rock-cut churches and valleys, is a good starting point for a first visit. "Göreme National Park and the Rock Sites of Cappadocia" has been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 1985.

A first three-day trip

Day 1: watch the balloons at sunrise, then visit the Göreme Open-Air Museum, a monastic settlement about 2 km east of the village, used from the 4th to the 13th century; don't miss the Tokalı Church in the museum area. Late afternoon: Zemi Valley. Day 2: walk through Pigeon Valley to Uçhisar and climb Uçhisar Castle; sunset in the Rose and Red valleys. Day 3: Love Valley in the morning, a quiet village afternoon; if you like, pottery in Avanos or an underground city.

Balloons

On mornings when flights are permitted, many balloons rise over the valleys at sunrise. Flights depend on the weather: if you plan to fly, book the first morning of your trip so later days remain as backups. Even without flying, you can watch them from Göreme or Uçhisar.

Good to know

Valley trails can be poorly marked; ask before you set off. Bring water and a hat. The rock is soft: don't climb the fairy chimneys or leave marks that could damage the rock or historic surfaces. Don't touch the frescoes. People live in the villages, so keep noise down. Emergency number: 112.

Opening hours, transport and event details can change; check with the relevant institution before you go.
