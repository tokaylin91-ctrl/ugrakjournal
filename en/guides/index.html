Travel guides

  • How many days do you need in Safranbolu?

    One day is enough for Çarşı, the old bazaar quarter. Allow two days to add the museums, mansions and Kıranköy, and three days for the İncekaya Aqueduct, the Crystal Terrace and Yörük Village.

  • Sinop or Amasra?

    Choose Amasra for a small, fully walkable harbour town and a short getaway. Choose Sinop for a longer stay that combines history, museums and nature routes such as coves, a lagoon and waterfalls. With more time, you can combine both on a road trip following the Black Sea coast; it is a long drive, so don't rush it.

  • Cappadocia for first-time visitors

    For a first visit, base yourself around Göreme and Uçhisar and allow at least three days: one for the rock churches and the Open-Air Museum, one for the Pigeon Valley walk to Uçhisar, and one for Love Valley and a quiet village afternoon. Balloons depend on the weather; bring water and a hat, and never climb the fairy chimneys.
