Help & FAQ

How do I open my journal?

Your personal code appears on screen right after payment and is emailed to you. Open the journal's address on your phone, enter the code, then use "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone with Safari and Android with Chrome. You need internet the first time; after that the journal also opens offline.

How many devices can I use?

One code works on up to 2 devices. Codes are personal and cannot be shared or transferred.

My code didn't arrive or I lost it.

Check your spam folder first. If it isn't there, write to info@ugrakjournal.com or WhatsApp us with your order number and we'll resend it.

Is it a PDF or an App Store app?

Neither. It is a web app (PWA) that opens in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen.

What languages are included?

City journals are in Turkish and English, including the audio stories. The children's journals (Martı) are Turkish only.

Refunds

The journals are digital content delivered immediately after payment. If your code doesn't work or the journal won't open, we fix it first; if we can't, we refund you. Full terms (in Turkish) are on each journal's information page.

Payment & currency

Payments are processed securely through Shopier. Prices are displayed in Turkish lira (TRY). Your bank or card provider may automatically convert the amount into your local currency; exchange rates and any foreign transaction fees are set by your payment provider. Your card details are never shared with us.

Uğrak is based in Sinop, Türkiye. Your purchase is a digital product; no physical item is shipped. Support is available in English by email.
