How to buy and install

Choose, pay on Shopier, get your code, add the journal to your home screen. It takes a few minutes.

  1. Choose your journalTap "Add to cart" on a city page. You can pick several journals: you pay once, and one code opens all of them.
  2. Pay on Shopier"Checkout" takes you to Shopier's secure payment page. Your card details stay with Shopier; we never see them.
  3. Your code: on screen and by emailWhen payment is complete, your personal code appears on screen. It is also sent to the email address you gave at checkout. If you can't see the email, check your spam folder; if you still can't find your code, write to us and we'll help right away.
  4. Tap the button in the emailOpen the email on your phone and tap "Open your journal".
  5. Add it to your home screenIn Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom (square with an up arrow) and choose "Add to Home Screen".In Chrome, tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right and choose "Add to home screen" or "Install app".
  6. Tap the new iconThe journal now sits on your home screen like an app.
  7. Enter your code and open itYou enter the code once. After the first load the journal works offline too, on up to 2 devices.
