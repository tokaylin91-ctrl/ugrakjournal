How many days do you need in Safranbolu?

Short answer: One day is enough for Çarşı, the old bazaar quarter. Allow two days to add the museums, mansions and Kıranköy, and three days for the İncekaya Aqueduct, the Crystal Terrace and Yörük Village.

1 day: Çarşı

Start early at the Arasta and watch the craftsmen as the shops open. Then visit Cinci Inn (1645) and the Cinci Bath next to it. The Köprülü Mehmet Pasha Mosque (1661) and the İzzet Mehmet Pasha Mosque stand in the middle of Çarşı. In the late afternoon climb Hıdırlık Hill and see the rooftops catch the light.

2 days: the town's memory

On day two, begin with the clock tower (1797) and the Safranbolu Museum beside it, housed in the old government building of 1904–1906. The Kaymakamlar House is open as a house museum; check opening times before you go. In the afternoon wander the streets of Kıranköy, once home to a Greek Orthodox community and much changed by the 1924 population exchange.

3 days: canyon and village

On day three, drive to the Tokatlı Canyon. The İncekaya Aqueduct was built by İzzet Mehmet Pasha in the 18th century; it has six arches and rises up to about 60 metres above the canyon. The nearby Crystal Terrace looks down into the canyon from about 80 metres. On the way back, stop at Yörük Village and at Bağlar, the town's historic summer quarter.

Practical notes

Çarşı is explored on foot; the streets are cobbled and steep, so wear comfortable shoes. You need a car for the canyon and villages. Some mansions are still homes, so keep your voice down. Stay away from the edge in the canyon and on terraces; children should be with an adult.
